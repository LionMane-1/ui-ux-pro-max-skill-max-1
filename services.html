Dry cleaning

Suits, coats, jackets, trousers, knitwear, silks and tailoring. Cleaned in a closed-loop hydrocarbon machine that lifts soil without stripping the oils that keep wool alive.

  • Fibre-sorted, not colour-sortedCashmere never shares a load with a raincoat.
  • Pre-spotted by handFourteen reagents on the bench, matched to the actual stain.
  • Finished on shaped formsShoulders keep their line; lapels keep their roll.
  • Two-piece suits cleaned togetherAlways. Cleaning half a suit is how suits stop matching.
