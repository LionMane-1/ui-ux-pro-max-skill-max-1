## Logo
- Refined from Concept 1 (Option A). Personality: trusted, warm, premium, modern, distinctive.
- Colours: dark espresso brown, copper/bronze, white for "AH", warm cream background.
- Variants: on dark, on light, and a small-size app icon / TikTok profile crop.
- Subtle Afro-hair-growth cue in the swoosh; works at avatar size.

## Voice summary (from the framework)
- **Positioning:** specialist growth partner and digital infrastructure architect for UK Afro hair salons. Turns Instagram attention into deposit-paid bookings.
- **Audience:** overwhelmed independent salon owners (primary) and ambitious mobile/freelance stylists (secondary).
- **Core problem:** the conversion and trust gap ("DM for price", no-shows, weak local search).
- **Voice attributes:** Culturally Fluent, Radically Clear, Warmly Authoritative, Outcome-Obsessed, Empathetic but Boundaried.
- **Taglines:** "Visibility. Trust. Bookings." / "Stop Posting. Start Booking." / "The Growth Partner for Afro Hair Specialists."
- **Pillars:** Trust is the Currency; Friction Kills Revenue; Visibility Beyond Vanity; The Value of Time; Professionalism as a Premium; Proof Beats Promises.
- **Avoid:** marketing fluff (synergy, paradigm shift), forced slang or tokenism, blame language, overpromising.
- **Governance:** 10-point QA checklist, 5-step approval workflow and red-flag watchlist in sections 9.1-9.3 of the PDF.
