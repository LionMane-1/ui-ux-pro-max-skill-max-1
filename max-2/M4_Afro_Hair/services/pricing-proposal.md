# M4 AH Pricing Proposal (DRAFT v2 - market tested)

**Important:** v1 figures were my working proposal. v2 below is tested against published UK benchmarks (see "Market test" at the end). Those benchmarks come mostly from agency blogs, so they are indicative only. Still get 3-5 real competitor quotes before publishing.

## Proposed prices (GBP, excl. VAT if registered)

| Package | Setup (one-off) | Monthly | Minimum term |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Booking Foundation | £350 | £99 | Rolling monthly |
| 2. Local Visibility | £450 | £249 | 3 months, then rolling |
| 3. Full Growth Partner | £750 | £449 | 3 months, then rolling |

v1 -> v2 changes: Package 2 monthly £149 -> £249 and Package 3 monthly £299 -> £449 (both were below market). Package 1 and all setup fees unchanged.

Bundle logic: Package 3 costs less than 1 + 2 bought separately (£800 setup, £348 monthly - saves £50 a month on the monthly fee), and adds reactivation, sequences and content planning.

## Why these numbers
- **Payback is simple to explain.** The framework's core proof is "two filled slots a month covers the fee". Published Afro hair salon price lists show box braids at about £95-£230 depending on length, so an average booking of about £140 is realistic (confirm with your first clients). At that value Package 3 at £449 pays for itself with about 3 extra bookings a month, Package 2 at £249 with 2, and Package 1 at £99 with 1.
- **Low entry price.** Package 1 is the "safe first step" for sceptical owners and a clear upgrade path to 2 and 3.
- **Setup fees cover real work.** Booking page, policies, Google profile and sequences take hours to build; charging setup stops us giving that away.
- **Longer minimum on SEO and growth packages.** Local search takes weeks to move. Package 1 stays rolling because it is quick to deliver and quick to prove.

## Software costs
Clients hold their own accounts for booking, email/SMS and Google, so **they own every asset**. Software subscriptions are billed by the provider direct to the client at cost (typically a low monthly fee; confirm current provider prices). Our fee covers setup and management only. This fits the "assets you own forever" message.

## Guarantee (recommended)
Offer a **delivery guarantee**, not an outcome guarantee:
> "If your booking page and Google profile are not live within 14 days of us receiving your photos and service list, your first month's fee is on us."

Why: we control delivery, and the framework's red-flag list warns against overpromising (for example "fully booked in 24 hours"). Avoid guaranteeing rankings or booking numbers.

## Payment terms
- Setup fee: 50% to start, 50% on go-live.
- Monthly fee: Direct Debit, same date each month.
- Late payment: paused service after 7 days, framed as policy, not punishment (framework language rule).

## Things to confirm before publishing
1. Competitor quotes (UK agencies and freelancers serving salons).
2. Your own cost per client (hours x rate) to check margin.
3. VAT status.
4. Pilot discount: for example 3 founding-client slots at 30% off setup in exchange for case study and testimonial rights. Strongly recommended, since the framework relies on proof and case studies.

Software cost is paid by the client direct, so total is about £115-£135 a month |
| Afro-specific agency | No UK agency found publishing Afro-salon marketing prices | - | Possible gap and differentiator, but not proven. Ask salons what they pay |

### Findings
1. **Package 2 and 3 monthly fees were too low.** Fixed in v2.
2. **Strong sales proof point:** marketplace platforms can take 20-35% commission on new clients. A salon taking a £140 braid booking through its own page keeps the full amount (less card fees). Use this in sales conversations for Package 1.
3. **Setup fees are sound.** £350-£750 sits inside the £300-£800 range for one-off setup.
4. **Our fees are low against generic agencies** (£500-£1,000+ a month for social alone). That is fine for micro-business clients, but leaves little room for discounts. The founding-client discount should apply to setup fees only.
5. **Gap in the data:** I found no published prices from agencies serving Afro hair salons. Treat the "no direct competitor" point as unverified.

### Caveats
- Almost all sources are agency or software-vendor blogs with an interest in their own pricing, and they disagree with each other.
- Software prices have changed several times in 2025-26. Check each provider's pricing page before quoting.
- Some figures come from US sources (USD) and were not used.
