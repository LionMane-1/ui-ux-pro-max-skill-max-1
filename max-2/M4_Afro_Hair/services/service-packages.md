# M4 AH Service Packages (DRAFT)

Status: draft for review. Prices are placeholders (£X) here. See `pricing-proposal.md` for the proposed figures awaiting approval.
Built from the Brand Voice Framework (`brand/voice/`): benefit first, plain English, prices and deliverables stated upfront, no overpromising.

## Positioning
We turn your Instagram likes into fully booked chairs. Setup fee once, then a monthly management fee. No "DM for price", for you or for us.

## Who each package is for
| Package | Best for | Main problem solved |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Booking Foundation | Salon owners and mobile stylists who lose evenings to DMs | "DM for price" and no-shows |
| 2. Local Visibility | Salons with a good booking path that clients can't find on Google | Not being found by clients ready to book |
| 3. Full Growth Partner | Established salons ready to hand over marketing admin | Empty slots, admin overload, reliance on Instagram alone |

## 1. Booking Foundation
Stop the late-night DM ping-pong. Clients see your work, your prices and your prep guide, then pay a deposit without messaging you.

**Includes**
- Online booking page with clear service menu and prices
- Deposit and cancellation policy set up and displayed
- Automatic confirmation and reminder emails/SMS
- "How to prep your hair" and "What to expect" guide sent after booking
- Booking link set up for Instagram bio, WhatsApp and Google

**Fees:** setup £X, then £X per month for support and updates.
**Time to go live:** to be confirmed.

## 2. Local Visibility
Be found by clients searching "knotless braids [your area]", not just scrolled past on Explore.

**Includes**
- Google Business Profile setup and optimisation
- Service-specific local search targeting (for example silk press, locs, braids, takedown)
- Review request flow to build reviews
- Mobile-friendly service pages or website updates
- Monthly visibility report: searches, profile views, booking clicks

**Fees:** setup £X, then £X per month.

## 3. Full Growth Partner
Everything in 1 and 2, plus the follow-up that keeps your diary full.

**Includes**
- Everything in Booking Foundation and Local Visibility
- Database reactivation campaign to past clients (one-off at start)
- Email/SMS sequences: rebooking reminders, aftercare, win-back
- Monthly content plan tied to services you want to fill
- Monthly report on bookings, deposits and revenue, plus a strategy call

**Fees:** setup £X, then £X per month.

## Add-ons (priced separately)
| Add-on | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Content day (photo/video of your work) | A month of posts that show your finishes |
| Reel and caption writing | Posts that pre-qualify clients and point to your booking link |
| Extra staff or location setup | Each stylist gets their own booking calendar |

## How we report
We report **bookings confirmed, deposits paid and revenue generated**. Likes, reach and views are shown only when tied to bookings.
