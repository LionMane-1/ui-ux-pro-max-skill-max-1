Small print, said plainly

How the money works

After inspection, not at booking. The estimate you see when you book is a starting figure based on the services you chose. Once your pieces are counted and checked at the atelier, you get an itemised total to approve. Nothing is taken from a card until you have seen that number.

Because a plain lambskin jacket and a heavily embellished vintage shearling are not the same job. For leather, bridal, restoration and rugs we quote after we have the piece in front of us. If the quote is more than you want to spend, we return it, cleaned or not, at no charge.

It rolls forward for three months. After that it expires — we say so here rather than in a footnote. If you are consistently carrying credit, we will suggest you move down a tier. We would rather keep you for ten years than overcharge you for one.

No minimum. One shirt is a perfectly good reason to call a van. Baskets under £30 carry a £5.90 collection and delivery charge for the whole round trip, and members pay nothing at any basket size.
