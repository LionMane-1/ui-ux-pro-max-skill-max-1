# M4 AH — Instagram Pre-Outreach Warm-Up (Foot in the Door)
Drafted 9 October 2026; revised same day. Built on the v2.0 decision register. No publishing, DMs or ad spend are authorised by this file.

**Scope of this phase: not selling.** No calculator, no demos pushed, no offers, no prices. The only job is to be a credible, useful peer so that a later conversation is welcome. The calculator and conversion funnel in the v2.0 strategy are deliberately parked for a later phase.

## Objective
When a salon owner opens your DM and taps your profile, they should see in under 10 seconds: **who you help, a problem they recognise, and proof you understand their day.** The goal is a reply and a conversation, not a sale.

**Gate to start DMs (all must be true):** profile live, 9 grid posts published, 2 Highlights live, disclosure pinned, and a named person who replies to DMs and comments within a day.

## 6. Warm-up engagement protocol (manual, ~20 min a day, days 1–7)
Goal: appear in a target's notifications 3–4 times as a helpful peer before any DM.
1. Build a list of 40 London-first Afro/textured-hair salon owners (problem-aware segment). Log handle, location, service mix, whether they take bookings by DM.
2. Daily, per 8–10 accounts: follow, then like 2 recent posts, view their Stories, and leave **one specific, non-promotional comment** (reference the style, the result or their service). No emojis-only comments, no links.
3. Reply to Story polls from the target when they use them.
4. Do **not** use automation, bulk follow tools or copied comments. They breach platform rules and put the account at risk.
5. Anyone who views your Stories or follows back moves to the DM list.

## 7. DM sequence (only after the gate and 3+ touches)
Keep each message under 40 words. One ask per message. No links in message 1.

**Message 1 (permission-based, reference something real):**
> Hi [name], loved the [specific style/post]. Quick question from one hair-business person to another: when a client DMs after hours, who usually replies, you or the team?

**Message 2 (after a reply: listen, add one useful thing, no offer):**
> Thanks, that's really common. One thing that helps is comparing booked vs available hours by weekday. Happy to share how I do it if useful. What's your quietest day?

**Message 3 (keep it a conversation; one nudge only if no reply after 3 days):**
> No rush at all. If it's helpful, I'm keen to hear how you handle enquiries when you're busy with a client.

If they ask what you do, answer honestly in one line and let them lead on whether to talk further. Do not introduce pricing, packages or a booking link in this phase.

**Stop rules:** one nudge, then stop. No reply after the nudge = remove from list. Never send unsolicited bulk messages.

## 8. What to measure in the first 7 days
| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Profile visits from targeted accounts | Is the warm-up working? |
| Follow-backs from the 40 list | Warmth |
| Story poll replies | Research and DM hooks |
| DM replies / DMs sent | Message quality (blank denominator until DMs start) |
| Conversations of 3+ messages | Real relationship signal |
| Unprompted "what do you do?" questions | Readiness for a later phase |

Set targets only after baseline data exists. Historical engagement percentages are not validated targets.

## 9. Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Template slides repeat across posts | High | Rewrite slides 4, 6, 7, 8 on the 9 seed posts |
| Content reads as a disguised sales pitch | High | No links, offers or tools; practical-check CTAs only |
| Over-promising results | High | Say "illustrative"; no payback guarantees |
| Account flagged for outreach behaviour | Medium | Manual, low volume, personalised, no copy-paste |
| No one owns lead replies | Medium | Name a response owner and cover hours before the DMs begin |
| Pricing conflict in older records | Low for now | Nothing priced in this phase |
