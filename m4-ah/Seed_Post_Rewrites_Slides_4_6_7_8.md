# Slides 4, 6, 7, 8 — rewrites for the nine IG seed posts
Drafted 9 October 2026. Replaces the shared template text. Slides 1, 2, 3 and 5 are unchanged. No selling, links, prices or calculator. Word counts are headline + body (limit 40, label included).

## Busy Isn’t Booked — `m4ah_w01_d01_carousel`
**Slide 4** (23 words)  
HEADLINE: Tuesday stays quiet  
BODY: Saturday fills, so the week feels good. Nobody checks the other days, so Tuesday’s empty chairs go unnoticed. Illustrative example.  
ARTIFACT: Diary page: Saturday packed, Tuesday blank; Keisha glancing only at Saturday.

**Slide 6** (21 words)  
HEADLINE: Try it on one week  
BODY: List each day’s booked hours beside its open hours. Circle the day with the biggest difference.  
ARTIFACT: Pen circling one weekday on a printed week grid.

**Slide 7** (24 words)  
HEADLINE: Now you can see it  
BODY: A full day no longer hides a thin one. Keisha can offer Tuesday slots to regulars instead of guessing.  
ARTIFACT: Before: one busy day in focus. After: whole week visible.

**Slide 8** (18 words)  
HEADLINE: Check one week  
BODY: Busy tells you Saturday went well. Booked hours tell you how the whole week went.  
ARTIFACT: ACTION: Check one Tuesday in your diary today.
