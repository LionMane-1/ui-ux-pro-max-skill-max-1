How it works

Four steps, none of them yours

Book in ninety seconds. We do the rest — including remembering how you like your collars, every time after the first.

  1. Book a window

    Pick a day and a four-hour window. Same-day collection is available before 11:00, Monday to Saturday.

  2. We collect

    A driver arrives with a reusable garment bag. Nothing to print, no packaging to find, no waiting in.

  3. The atelier

    Every piece is inspected, tagged, treated for stains and matched to the right process before it is cleaned.

  4. Back on your rail

    Returned in 48 hours, pressed, on wooden hangers, with a note of anything we found and fixed.

The difference

A machine cannot read a garment. A person can.

Most volume cleaners sort by colour and run one cycle. We sort by fibre, construction, dye stability and what the stain actually is — because a cashmere coat and a linen suit have nothing in common but a hanger.

  • Inspected by hand, twice Once on arrival and once before it leaves. Buttons, seams, linings, pockets — all of it.
  • Stains treated individually Fourteen reagents on the spotting bench. Red wine, ink and olive oil are three different problems.
  • Finished, not just dried Hand pressing on shaped forms so shoulders keep their line and lapels keep their roll.
  • Told the truth If a stain will not come out, we say so before we start — and we do not charge you to find out.

Questions

Before you book

We tell you before we clean, not after. If our spotter judges a mark permanent, you get a call or a message with the options — attempt it, leave it, or have the piece re-dyed. If an attempted removal fails, there is no charge for that garment.

Standard return is 48 hours. Express is 24 hours for a 40% supplement, and same-day is available on collections booked before 11:00 within our core districts. Bridal, leather and restoration work is quoted individually — those are never rushed.

A hydrocarbon solvent in a closed-loop machine, plus professional wet cleaning for anything that will take water. We removed perchloroethylene from the building in 2016. Nothing leaves the atelier smelling of chemicals, and members can request a fragrance-free finish as standard.

No. Leave your bag with a concierge, a neighbour, or in a safe place you describe when booking. You will get a photograph when it is collected and another when it is returned.

Every garment is insured to £15,000 from the moment it leaves your door. In twenty-seven years we have made forty-one claims. We settle at replacement value, not depreciated value, and we do it without an argument.
